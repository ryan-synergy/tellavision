Privacy Policy

TellaVision · Synergy AV · Updated 4 September 2026

TellaVision collects no data. None at all. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services of any kind.

What the app stores

Everything you create stays on your device:

  • Your wall dimensions, TV selection, mount and back-box choices
  • Annotations and measurements you draw
  • Any reference drawing (PDF or image) you import
  • Any corrections you make to the product catalog

This is held in the app's own on-device storage. It is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. Every component it needs is bundled inside it, and it works fully with the network switched off.

Files you export

When you export a drawing — PDF, DXF, JSON, PNG or SVG — the file is created on your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice. The app has no visibility into it.

Files you import

Importing a reference drawing uses the standard iOS document picker. The app can only read the single file you pick, and that file is stored on-device with everything else.

Permissions

TellaVision requests no permissions. It does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photo library, or health data.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Given the app collects nothing, material change is unlikely.
