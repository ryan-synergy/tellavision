Common questions

How do I trace over an architect's drawing?
IMPORT → Reference drawing, then Snap to TV: drag a box over the TV drawn on the elevation and it scales and positions the sheet in one move. Use 2-point scale if the drawing has no TV — click both ends of a known dimension and type its real length.

The architect's drawing is cluttered.
Crop limits the sheet to the wall you care about without touching your calibration. Blank paints over anything left inside the working area.

The text on the drawing is too small.
Settings (gear icon) → Text size → S / M / L / XL.

A dimension looks wrong.
Every back box and mount dimension is traced to a manufacturer document. If you believe one is wrong, email the model and the spec sheet — corrections are taken seriously. You can also fix it yourself under DATA, and your change is kept separate from the shipped catalog.

Always verify before drilling. Dimensions are calculated from published specifications and can vary by model variant. Check the VESA pattern and panel dimensions against the manufacturer's spec sheet.
